# stusy

A study secretary that runs inside the AI you already use: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok or any other chat.

Give it your syllabuses and lecture notes. You get back:

- every deadline on one calendar, plus a `.ics` file for your phone
- your week ranked by what protects the most marks
- what can safely get the minimum
- the mark you need on the final to hit your target grade
- a revision mode that explains topics and quizzes you from your own notes

**Try it (free, no account):** open [stusy](https://github.com/stusyweb-bit/stusy), copy the grey box in `PORTABLE.md`, paste it into a new AI chat and type `stusy`.
